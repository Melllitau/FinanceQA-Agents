<h1 align="center">
    On the Potential of Tool-Enhanced Small <br> Language Models to Match Large Models in Finance
</h1>

<p align="justify">
This repository contains the code and additional resources associated with the paper 
<b><i>On the Potential of Tool-Enhanced Small Language Models to Match Large Models in Finance</i></b>, 
accepted for publication at <a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3768292.3770409">The 6th ACM International Conference on AI in Finance (ICAIF ’25)</a>.
</p>
